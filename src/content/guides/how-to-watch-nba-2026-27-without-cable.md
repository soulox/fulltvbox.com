## The short version

The 2026-27 NBA season started on **October 20** and runs to **April 11, 2027**. It's the second year of the league's new TV deals, and national games are split three ways:

| Partner | Main nights | How to stream |
|---|---|---|
| **NBC / Peacock** | Tuesdays (doubleheaders), Mondays (Peacock only), Sunday Night Basketball late in the season, the All-Star Game | Peacock Premium, $12.99/mo; NBC also free with an antenna |
| **ESPN / ABC** | Wednesdays on ESPN, Saturday primetime and some Sundays on ABC, Christmas Day, the NBA Finals | ESPN Unlimited, $31.99/mo; ABC also free with an antenna |
| **Prime Video** | Fridays (doubleheaders), late-season Thursdays, the NBA Cup knockouts, the Play-In, the Eastern Conference Finals | Amazon Prime ($14.99/mo) or Prime Video alone ($8.99/mo) |

**No single subscription has all of it.** Prices are US list prices as of October 21, 2026.

## What's on each service

### NBC and Peacock

NBC opened the season with a tripleheader and has **Coast 2 Coast Tuesday** doubleheaders through the season. **Sunday Night Basketball** takes over once football winds down, now 16 games, and NBC also has the Martin Luther King Jr. Day and Presidents' Day tripleheaders, "Holiday Hoops" doubleheaders on December 22, 28 and 29, and the **All-Star Game** on February 21, 2027 in Phoenix.

Peacock streams every NBC game, plus **Monday night games that are only on Peacock**. You need **Peacock Premium ($12.99/month)** or Premium Plus ($19.99/month, which removes ads except during live events); the cheaper Peacock Select plan doesn't include live sports.

### ESPN and ABC

ESPN has **Wednesday night** games, the five-game **Christmas Day** slate (shared with ABC) and the final night of the regular season. ABC has **Saturday primetime** games from December to March and Sunday afternoon games in late February and March, then the **NBA Finals** in June. This season ESPN/ABC also has the Western Conference Finals.

To stream ESPN without cable you need **ESPN Unlimited ($31.99/month or $319.99/year)**. The cheaper ESPN Select tier doesn't include the ESPN channel's NBA games; our [ESPN Select vs. Unlimited guide](/guides/espn-select-vs-unlimited-2026) breaks down the difference. ABC games are free over the air.

### Prime Video

Amazon has **Friday night doubleheaders** for most of the season, including most NBA Cup group-stage nights, then **Thursday** games from January to April and some Saturday afternoon games. It also has the **entire NBA Cup knockout round** (the final moves to Indianapolis this year), all **Play-In** games, and the **Eastern Conference Finals**. Because Christmas falls on a Friday this year, Prime's holiday doubleheader moves to Wednesday, December 23.

Any Amazon Prime membership includes it, or Prime Video is $8.99/month on its own.

## Your local team

Local games are the messy part. Depending on your team, they air on a regional sports network, a team-owned streaming app, or local broadcast TV, and the arrangements have changed a lot in the past two seasons. Check your team's website for "how to watch"; it will name the service for your area.

League Pass won't help here: your local team is blacked out on it live.

---

## NBA League Pass: for out-of-market fans

If you follow a team in another city, **League Pass** carries its games. NBA.com's US prices in October 2026:

- **League Pass:** $16.99/month or $109.99 for the season (with ads, one device)
- **League Pass Premium:** $24.99/month or $159.99 for the season (no ads, three devices, downloads)
- **Team Pass** (one team): $13.99/month

The catch: **national games are blacked out** (ESPN, ABC, NBC, Peacock and Prime Video), as are your local team's games. Those appear in League Pass from 6am ET the next day. The NBA can change prices during the season, so check NBA.com before buying.

## The cheapest ways to watch

**Every national game, no cable:** an antenna (free, for ABC and NBC) + ESPN Unlimited ($31.99) + Peacock Premium ($12.99) + Prime Video ($8.99) = **about $54/month**. That's cheaper than a live TV service and covers more games, because live TV services don't include Peacock's Monday games or Prime's games anyway.

**Casual fan:** an antenna plus Peacock Premium gets you NBC's Tuesday and Sunday games, the All-Star Game and ABC's Saturday games and Finals for **$12.99/month**. Add Prime if you already have it.

**Big-game-only:** an antenna alone covers ABC's Saturday games, Christmas on ABC, NBC's games and the Finals for **$0**. Our [antenna DVR tutorial](/tutorials/ota-tv-dvr-hdhomerun-jellyfin) shows how to record them and watch on any TV in the house.

**Cancel in the off-season:** all of these are monthly. Subscribe in October, cancel in June, and don't pay for summer.
