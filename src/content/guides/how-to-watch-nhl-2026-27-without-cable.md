## The short version

The 2026-27 NHL season started on **September 29** and runs to **April 10, 2027**, now **84 games per team**, the most since 1993-94. In the US, national games are shared between two partners:

| Partner | What it has | How to stream |
|---|---|---|
| **ESPN / ABC** | ABC's Hockey Saturday (16 games, January–April), ESPN Tuesday/Thursday games, Sunday doubleheaders, the All-Star Game and Stadium Series on ABC | ESPN Unlimited ($31.99/mo) for ESPN; ABC free with an antenna |
| **ESPN streaming** | 47 streaming-only games plus out-of-market games (NHL Power Play) | ESPN Select ($13.99/mo); the 47 games also stream on Hulu and Disney+ |
| **TNT** | Mostly Wednesday nights, plus the Winter Classic, the Thanksgiving Showdown, Sunday games and the **Stanley Cup Final** | HBO Max Standard ($18.49/mo) or Premium |

Prices are US list prices as of October 21, 2026. Local games are separate and depend on your team; see below.

## ESPN and ABC

**ABC** carries **Hockey Saturday**, 16 regular-season games from January to April, plus the **All-Star Game** (February 7, 2027) and the **Stadium Series** (February 20, 2027, Stars vs. Golden Knights in Arlington, Texas). All free over the air with an antenna.

**ESPN** has select Tuesday and Thursday games and Sunday doubleheaders late in the season. Streaming the ESPN channel needs **ESPN Unlimited** ($31.99/month or $319.99/year).

**ESPN Select** ($13.99/month or $139.99/year) is the cheaper tier, and for many hockey fans it's the better buy: it has **47 games that stream only on ESPN**, mostly Tuesdays and Thursdays, and **NHL Power Play**, the out-of-market package with over a thousand games. Those 47 exclusive games are also simulcast on Hulu and Disney+. Our [ESPN Select vs. Unlimited guide](/guides/espn-select-vs-unlimited-2026) explains the two tiers in full.

## TNT and HBO Max

**TNT** has most **Wednesday night** games, select Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday games, the **Thanksgiving Showdown**, a Martin Luther King Jr. Day doubleheader, the **Winter Classic** (December 31, Mammoth vs. Avalanche in Salt Lake City), and this season's **Stanley Cup Final**.

Without cable, **HBO Max** streams every TNT game on its ad-free plans: Standard ($18.49/month) or Premium ($22.99/month). The cheaper Basic with Ads plan doesn't include them.

## Playoffs

The first two rounds are split between ESPN and TNT. ESPN picks one conference final and TNT gets the other, then **TNT has the Stanley Cup Final** in June 2027. With both ESPN Unlimited and HBO Max (or a live TV service with ESPN, ABC and TNT), you'll see every round.

## Your local team

Local broadcasts changed a lot this year. FanDuel Sports Network stopped carrying NHL games after last season, and teams moved to a mix of broadcast TV, team apps and new streaming partners. Some examples:

- **Prime Video:** Amazon now has the regional rights for the **Anaheim Ducks** and **Dallas Stars**, so Prime members in those markets get local games. The **Blue Jackets, Blues, Hurricanes and Wild** use Amazon to distribute their own team streaming services, sold separately and without needing Prime.
- **DAZN:** replaces the Gotham Sports app for **MSG Network** teams (Rangers, Islanders, Devils, Sabres), and also carries the **LA Kings**.
- **Detroit Red Wings:** Detroit SportsNet, streaming on MLB.tv.
- **Nashville Predators:** free over the air on local broadcast TV (WNPX).

Arrangements vary by team and can change, so check your team's website for its "how to watch" page; it names the service for your area. Your local team is blacked out on NHL Power Play.

## The cheapest ways to watch

**Following a team from another city:** ESPN Select ($13.99/month) covers most of its games through NHL Power Play, plus the 47 exclusives. Add HBO Max ($18.49) for TNT's national games, which are blacked out on Power Play. **About $32/month.**

**Every national game:** ESPN Unlimited ($31.99) + HBO Max Standard ($18.49) + an antenna for ABC = **about $50/month**. That's still less than a live TV service like YouTube TV ($82.99/month).

**Big games only:** an antenna gets you ABC's Saturday games, the All-Star Game and the Stadium Series for **$0**. Add HBO Max for a month or two in spring for the Stanley Cup Final.
