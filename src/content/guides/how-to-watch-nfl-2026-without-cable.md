The 2026 season is past its midpoint. Here's where the rest of it is:

| Games | Where | Cheapest way to stream |

## Sundays: CBS, Fox and NBC

Most games are still on regular broadcast TV. **CBS and Fox** split the Sunday afternoon games, and you get the ones picked for your local market. **NBC** has **Sunday Night Football** every week.

That makes an **antenna** the best value in football: all three networks, in full quality, for nothing after the antenna itself. Some NBC games are even broadcast in 1080p HDR on the newer ATSC 3.0 (NextGen TV) signal, if your TV or tuner supports it. Want to record games and watch on every TV? Our [antenna DVR tutorial](/tutorials/ota-tv-dvr-hdhomerun-jellyfin) sets that up.

If an antenna won't work where you live, each network has a streaming route:

- **CBS:** Paramount+ (Essential, $8.99/month with ads) streams your local CBS games.
- **Fox:** Fox One ($19.99/month) streams Fox's games.
- **NBC:** Peacock Premium ($12.99/month) streams Sunday Night Football. The cheaper Peacock Select plan doesn't include live sports.

## Monday Night Football: ESPN

Monday Night Football is on **ESPN**, and many games are simulcast on **ABC**. Streaming ESPN without cable needs **ESPN Unlimited** ($31.99/month or $319.99/year); the cheaper ESPN Select tier doesn't include it. Our [ESPN Select vs. Unlimited guide](/guides/espn-select-vs-unlimited-2026) has the details. ESPN and ABC also have this season's Super Bowl, the first one ever shown on ESPN.

## Thursday Night Football: Prime Video

Thursday Night Football streams on **Prime Video** (and Twitch). Any Amazon Prime membership includes it ($14.99/month or $139/year), or Prime Video alone is $8.99/month. Prime also has the **Black Friday** game (Steelers vs. Broncos, 3pm ET, November 27) and a **Christmas Eve** game.

## Netflix's games

Netflix is now a regular NFL broadcaster. Still to come this season:

- **Thanksgiving Eve**, Wednesday, November 25: Packers vs. Rams, the first game ever played that night
- **Christmas Day**, Friday, December 25: Packers at Bears (1pm ET) and Broncos vs. Bills (4:30pm ET)
- **Week 18**, Saturday, January 9, 2027: one 1pm ET game

Every Netflix plan includes them, including Standard with Ads at $8.99/month. Subscribe for a month around the holidays if you don't already have it.

## Other games to know

- **Thanksgiving Day** (November 26): afternoon games on CBS and Fox, and a night game on NBC, all free over the air.
- **NFL Network** has seven exclusive games, including the international games at 9:30am ET and two Saturday games on December 26. NFL Network comes with most live TV services and with NFL+ Premium.
- **Late-season Saturdays:** CBS/Fox games in Week 15 and NBC/Peacock games in Week 17, plus a Peacock-only primetime game on January 2, 2027.

## Out-of-market fans: Sunday Ticket

**NFL Sunday Ticket** carries every Sunday afternoon game from other markets, on YouTube or YouTube TV. Consumer Reports' September 2026 prices: **$480** for the season on its own (or eight payments of $60), **$378** with a YouTube TV subscription, and around **$240** as a new-subscriber offer. It doesn't include your local CBS/Fox games or any primetime game. Since the season is half over, check whether YouTube is offering a reduced late-season price before you buy.

**NFL+** is the cheaper alternative for following games rather than watching them on the TV: Basic (about $7/month) shows live local and primetime games on phones and tablets only; Premium (about $15/month) adds NFL Network, RedZone and full game replays.

## Do you need a live TV service?

YouTube TV ($82.99/month), Hulu + Live TV and Fubo (both around $90) include CBS, Fox, NBC, ESPN and NFL Network in one app. That's simplest, but it still doesn't include Prime's Thursday games or Netflix's. **Sling (Orange + Blue, about $61)** is cheaper but has no CBS.

For most households, **an antenna + Prime Video + one month of Netflix in December** covers everything except Monday nights for under $20 a month. Add ESPN Unlimited only if you won't miss Monday Night Football.
