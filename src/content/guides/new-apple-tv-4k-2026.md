## The short version

- **What launched:** a new **Apple TV 4K**, announced October 13, 2026, at Apple's "Welcome Home" event — shipping November 13, 2026, at **$299** for a single 128GB / Wi-Fi 7 / Gigabit Ethernet configuration.
- **The headline feature isn't the chip, it's the microphones.** Three built-in far-field mics let you talk to Siri without picking up the remote or owning a HomePod — a first for Apple TV.
- **Siri AI is coming to older boxes too.** The Apple Intelligence-powered Siri update is rolling out via tvOS 27.2 to the [current Apple TV 4K](/reviews/apple-tv-4k-3rd-gen) (2nd generation, 2022) as well, just without the hands-free microphones.
- **Who should care:** first-time Apple TV buyers, anyone who wants hands-free voice control, and gamers or heavy streamers who'll benefit from the A19 chip's extra GPU headroom and a new AV1 hardware decoder. Existing 2022-model owners who just stream are the group most early coverage says can sit this one out.

Apple hadn't meaningfully updated the Apple TV 4K's hardware since 2022, so a refresh was overdue — our own [Apple TV 4K review](/reviews/apple-tv-4k-3rd-gen) flagged it as coming back in the summer. Here's exactly what Apple shipped, and who it's actually for.

## What Apple actually changed

The new Apple TV 4K runs an **A19 chip**, which Apple says delivers up to 50% faster CPU performance and roughly twice the graphics performance of the outgoing A15-based model. For a device whose main job is decoding video and rendering a simple interface, that's more headroom than most people need day to day — but it does bring one genuinely useful addition: a hardware **AV1 decoder**, a format some streaming platforms (including YouTube) increasingly rely on and that the old chip couldn't accelerate.

Connectivity moves up too: **Wi-Fi 7**, **Bluetooth 6**, and a rear **HDMI 2.1** port. There's only one storage/connectivity tier this time — 128GB with Ethernet built in — rather than the separate Wi-Fi-only and Ethernet models Apple sold before.

None of that is why this launch is getting attention, though. The real story is what Apple built around Siri.

## Hands-free Siri, finally

Every previous Apple TV needed either a remote press or a paired HomePod to hear you. The new box has **three far-field microphones built in**, so you can just talk to it from across the room — the first time Apple TV has worked this way without extra hardware.

That's paired with **Siri AI**, Apple's Apple Intelligence-powered rework of Siri, which is debuting on Apple TV with this launch. At launch it's English-only, and limited to the US, Australia, Canada, India, New Zealand, Singapore, South Africa, and the UK. Requests that pull in personal context — your calendar, your messages, things specific to you — need a paired iPhone or iPad that itself supports Apple Intelligence; ask it something more generic ("find me a 90-minute comedy") and it doesn't need that pairing. We cover how this compares with Gemini on Google TV and Alexa+ on Fire TV in our [AI on your TV box guide](/guides/ai-on-tv-box-2026).

Here's the detail that changes the buying decision: **Siri AI isn't exclusive to the new hardware.** Apple is rolling the update out via a tvOS 27.2 beta to the Apple TV 4K (2nd generation) and later — meaning the 2022 model most people already own is getting the same AI assistant. The new box's advantage is purely the hands-free microphones; everything Siri AI can actually do is coming to older hardware too.

## The new remote

The redesigned Siri Remote ships with the new box and also sells separately for **$59**, working with any Apple TV 4K or Apple TV HD released since 2015. The headline addition is **Find My support**: the remote has a built-in speaker that can play a sound so you can locate it under a couch cushion, the same way Find My already works for AirTags and iPhones.

## What's NOT worth getting excited about

- **The faster chip, if you only stream.** Both the 2022 and 2026 boxes top out at 4K and 60fps for playback — the A19's extra CPU and GPU power matters for gaming and heavier on-device AI processing, not for watching Netflix.
- **Upgrading just for Siri AI.** It's coming to the box you may already own. Try it there first via the tvOS 27.2 beta before spending $299 on new hardware for the same assistant.
- **Expecting hands-free Siri on your current box.** The far-field microphones are new silicon — a software update can't retrofit them onto a 2022 unit. If always-listening voice control is the feature you actually want, that's the one thing only the new box delivers.
- **Treating $299 as a done deal if you don't stream in 4K already.** Cheaper boxes like the [Fire TV Stick 4K Max](/reviews/fire-tv-stick-4k-max-2024) or [Google TV Streamer](/reviews/google-tv-streamer-4k-2024) handle the same apps for a third of the price if you're not already committed to Apple's ecosystem — see our [best 4K HDR streaming devices guide](/guides/best-streaming-device-4k-hdr-dolby-vision-2026) for the wider field.

## Should you buy it?

**Buy it if** you're picking your first Apple TV, you want hands-free Siri without a HomePod in the room, you game on Apple TV and want the extra GPU headroom, or you've been holding off on the aging 2022 model specifically for an AI-capable Apple TV.

**Skip it if** you already own a 2022 Apple TV 4K and mostly just stream — wait for the tvOS 27.2 Siri AI update to land on your existing box, try it, and only upgrade the hardware if hands-free voice control specifically is worth $299 to you. If raw AI power matters more than the Apple ecosystem, our [AI on your TV box guide](/guides/ai-on-tv-box-2026) breaks down how Gemini on Google TV and Alexa+ on Fire TV compare.

Full third-party reviews haven't landed yet since the box doesn't ship until November 13 — treat early impressions, including ours, as based on Apple's own claims until independent testing confirms real-world performance.

## FAQ

**When does the new Apple TV 4K come out and what does it cost?** Announced October 13, 2026; ships November 13, 2026, at $299 for the single 128GB/Ethernet configuration.

**What's actually new?** An A19 chip, an AV1 hardware decoder, Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, three built-in far-field microphones for hands-free Siri, and a redesigned Siri Remote with Find My support (sold separately for $59).

**Does my current Apple TV get the new Siri AI too?** Yes — it's rolling out via the tvOS 27.2 beta to the 2022 Apple TV 4K and later, just without the new box's hands-free microphones.
