## Best Overall: NVIDIA Shield TV Pro

The Shield TV Pro remains the most powerful Android TV box available. It's the right choice if you run a Plex server, want AI upscaling, or need the fastest performance money can buy.

At $59, the Fire TV Stick 4K Max delivers 4K Dolby Vision and Atmos with Wi-Fi 6E. It's the easiest recommendation for most households.
