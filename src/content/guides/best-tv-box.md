## Best High-End Box: NVIDIA Shield TV Pro

The [Shield TV Pro](/reviews/nvidia-shield-tv-pro-2025) remains the most powerful Android TV box available. It's the right choice if you run a Plex server, want the best [AI upscaling](/guides/ai-video-upscaling-tv-box), or need the fastest performance money can buy.

## Best for Most People: Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K Max

At $59, the [Fire TV Stick 4K Max](/reviews/fire-tv-stick-4k-max-2024) delivers 4K Dolby Vision and Atmos with Wi-Fi 6E. It's the easiest recommendation for most households, and it's first in line for Amazon's new Alexa+ AI features.

**Price: ~$59** | [Check on Amazon →](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Amazon%20Fire%20TV%20Stick%204K%20Max%20(2024)&tag=fulltvbox-20)

## Best Budget Android TV Box: onn. 4K Pro

Walmart's [onn. 4K Pro](/reviews/onn-4k-pro-streaming-box) is the box to buy if you want Android TV (Google TV) without paying Shield money. For about $49 you get Dolby Vision, HDR10+, and Atmos plus the ports sticks leave out — Ethernet, a USB-A port for drives and adapters, and a backlit remote with a finder button. It's slower than the Shield and can't host a Plex server, but for streaming and light sideloading it's the best value box we've tested.

**Price: ~$49** | [Check on Amazon →](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Walmart%20onn.%204K%20Pro%20Streaming%20Box&tag=fulltvbox-20)

## Best for AI & Discovery: Google TV Streamer

The [Google TV Streamer](/reviews/google-tv-streamer-4k-2024) is the device leading Google's rollout of **Gemini** — conversational search, rich visual answers, and content recommendations across all your services. With Ethernet, 4GB of RAM, and Matter/Thread built in, it's the best all-round Google TV box.

**Price: ~$99** | [Check on Amazon →](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Google%20TV%20Streamer%20(4K)&tag=fulltvbox-20)

## Skip Unless You Only Use the Big Apps: Fire TV Stick 4K Select

Amazon's cheapest 4K stick at $40, the [Fire TV Stick 4K Select](/reviews/fire-tv-stick-4k-select-vega-os), is the first to run the new Linux-based **Vega OS**. It's snappy for mainstream streaming but drops Android — meaning **no sideloading**, a thinner app store, and no Dolby Vision. Fine as a basic appliance; the wrong choice for tinkerers.

## Want an Android TV box specifically?

"Android TV box" covers two very different things. **Name-brand boxes** — the Shield, the onn. 4K Pro, and the Google TV Streamer above — run Google-certified Android TV / Google TV, get security updates, and play Netflix and Prime Video in 4K. **Generic no-name boxes** sold on marketplaces often run uncertified Android builds that can't stream major apps in HD, rarely get updates, and sometimes ship with malware. We only recommend certified boxes: the onn. 4K Pro on a budget, the Google TV Streamer for the mainstream, and the Shield if you want power.

The hardware race has slowed; the real change this year is software. Every platform is adding generative AI — Gemini on Google TV, Alexa+ on Fire TV, and Apple Intelligence on the next Apple TV. Most of it is early, but a few features (conversational search, scene-jumping, AI upscaling) are genuinely useful.
