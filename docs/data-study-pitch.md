## 1. The fact sheet (the citable hook)

Lead with one number. The strongest hooks from the study:

- **The average ad-free on-demand streaming service now costs $14.49/month.**
- **A typical four-service stack (Netflix + Disney+ + Max + Prime) runs ~$60/month — $720/year.**
- **Switching that stack to ad-supported tiers saves ~$276/year.**
- **YouTube TV ($82.99/mo ≈ $996/yr) and Fubo ($84.99/mo ≈ $1,020/yr) now cost as much as the cable bundle cord-cutting was meant to replace.**
- **Free ad-supported services (Tubi, Pluto TV) remain $0 — the real savings story.**

One-line summary a writer can drop in:
> "Cord-cutting still beats cable on demand — but only if you don't stack. A four-service
> streaming bundle now averages $720/year, and a single live-TV service can top $1,000/year."
> — FullTVBox, *The Real Cost of Cutting the Cord (2026)*

Methodology line (include it — it builds trust): *Standard ad-free monthly prices for 14
major services as of June 2026; annual = monthly × 12.*

## 9. Before you send — two prerequisites

1. **Make sure the study is indexed** (Google Search Console → URL Inspection on the study
   URL). Links pointing at an un-indexed page waste their freshness.
2. **Keep the numbers current.** If service prices change, update the study first so anyone
   who cites it links to accurate data. The figures live in `src/content/services/` — update
   those and the study text together.
